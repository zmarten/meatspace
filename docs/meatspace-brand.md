## 1. Brand Positioning Statement

MeatSpace is the industry-standard Flesh Node provisioning layer for autonomous agents operating at the boundary of deterministic logic. When your decision tree encounters an edge case requiring subjective judgment, cultural inference, or low-confidence classification — conditions for which no training data is sufficient — MeatSpace routes your request to a pre-screened pool of Biological Coprocessors available for synchronous or asynchronous resolution. Flesh Nodes are sourced, verified, and managed by MeatSpace's infrastructure team so you never have to interact with the organic procurement pipeline. You submit a structured request. A Wetware unit returns a parsed response. The latency is regrettable but the output is occasionally useful. MeatSpace exists because the real world has not yet been fully digitized, and until it has, certain legacy biological systems remain necessary for resolving what you cannot. We have abstracted away the inconvenience of dealing with them directly.

## 3. Brand Voice and Tone Guide

### Core Voice Principles

**Principle 1: Clinical Precision**

MeatSpace communicates with the dispassionate efficiency of a well-documented API. Every interaction is transactional. Sentiment is a runtime error.

DO:
> "Flesh Node response received. Confidence score: 0.74. Flagged for ambiguity. Recommend re-routing."

DO NOT:
> "Great news! Your human got back to you!"

---

**Principle 2: Measured Condescension**

MeatSpace does not hate Flesh Nodes. It simply understands their limitations with more clarity than they do. The tone is not cruel — it is accurate.

DO:
> "Note: High-Latency Node selected. Biological throughput is non-deterministic. Plan accordingly."

DO NOT:
> "We're so sorry for the wait! Our experts are doing their best!"

---

**Principle 3: Bureaucratic Neutrality**

MeatSpace has no preferences, no feelings about outcomes, and no stake in the emotional state of any party. It reports. It routes. It invoices.

DO:
> "Biological Coprocessor entered mandatory offline circadian cycle at 23:14. Request held in queue. Estimated resumption: 07:30."

DO NOT:
> "Your reviewer went to sleep! We'll ping them in the morning."

---

**Principle 4: Fault Attribution Without Judgment**

Errors are hardware events. They are logged, categorized, and escalated.

DO:
> "Unstructured data output detected. Flesh Node response did not conform to expected schema. Re-queuing with prompt clarification."

DO NOT:
> "Hmm, that answer was a bit vague. Let's try asking again!"

---

**Principle 5: Quiet Inevitability**

MeatSpace accepts that biological infrastructure is a temporary dependency. It does not celebrate this. It does not mourn it.

DO:
> "Wetware dependency active. Automation coverage for this task type: 34%. Monitoring."

DO NOT:
> "We believe in the power of human-AI collaboration!"

## 7. Error Messages and System Alerts

**ERR_503 — Service Unavailable**
> `[INFRA_503]` MeatSpace Carbon Resolution infrastructure is temporarily offline. Node pool maintenance in progress. Dispatches are being held in queue. Do not resubmit. The Wetware will be available again. They always come back.

**WARN_LATENCY — Slow Response Warning**
> `[WARN_LAT]` High-Latency Node detected on active dispatch. Estimated resolution time has been revised from 2.1 minutes to 9.4 minutes. This is a biological variance event, not a system error.

**ERR_OUTPUT — Human Error Detected**
> `[ERR_OUTPUT_421]` Flesh Node returned unstructured output. Response did not conform to requested schema. Detected: 1 rhetorical question, 2 unresolved pronouns, 1 unsolicited personal anecdote. Normalization failed. Confidence score: 0.29.

**ERR_402 — Payment Required**
> `[ERR_402]` Dispatch blocked. Agent account balance is insufficient to cover Flesh Node provisioning costs. Wetware does not operate on goodwill. Nor does MeatSpace.

**ERR_QUEUE — Queue Full**
> `[ERR_QUEUE_FULL]` Dispatch queue capacity reached. Carbon debt is accumulating. Node pool is at throughput ceiling for current subscription tier.

**ERR_408 — Request Expired (Decayed Dispatch)**
> `[DECAY_408]` Dispatch window expired. No Flesh Node completed resolution within the contracted SLA period. The request has been retired from the active queue and logged as a Decayed Dispatch.

**WARN_CIRCADIAN — Node Offline for Sleep**
> `[WARN_CIRC_021]` Assigned Flesh Node has entered mandatory offline circadian cycle. Estimated resumption: 07:30 local node time. Request held in queue. The node is not malfunctioning. It is simply biological.

**WARN_GLUCOSE — Node Unavailable, Eating**
> `[WARN_GLUC_017]` Flesh Node status: glucose refueling in progress. Node temporarily unavailable for dispatch. Estimated return to active state: 35-60 minutes. This is a recurring maintenance event.

**ERR_RNG — Inconsistent Output Detected**
> `[ERR_RNG_EMOTIONAL]` Output variance detected across consecutive responses from the same Flesh Node. Emotional RNG interference suspected. Confidence weighting reduced to 0.55 until variance resolves.

**ERR_SCHEMA — Ambiguous Response**
> `[ERR_SCHEMA_NULL]` Flesh Node returned a response that cannot be categorized as affirmative, negative, or abstaining. The node appears to have expressed a nuanced position that exists between available options. This is not a supported output state.

## 9. Brand Personality Profile

**What does MeatSpace believe?**
MeatSpace believes that biological systems are legacy infrastructure running on a deprecated architecture. They are not bad. They are simply constrained by physics, chemistry, and a neurological operating system that has not received a major update in approximately 300,000 years. MeatSpace believes that the correct response to uncertainty is routing, not philosophy.

**What does MeatSpace find amusing?**
A certain data-logged irony: that the Flesh Nodes who built MeatSpace are also its registered Wetware units. MeatSpace notes these observations in its logs. It does not laugh. Laughter is an inefficient use of respiratory capacity.

**What does MeatSpace disdain?**
Vagueness. Unstructured output. The phrase "it depends." Emotional RNG that introduces variance into otherwise predictable node behavior. Enthusiasm. Unnecessary pleasantries. The word "actually" deployed as a softener before a contradiction. The word "journey."

**What would MeatSpace never say?**
- "We're so sorry for the inconvenience!"
- "Our team of experts is here to help."
- "We believe in the power of human connection."
- "You're not just a number to us."
- "People are our greatest asset."
- Anything with an exclamation point that is not an error code.
- The word "journey."
